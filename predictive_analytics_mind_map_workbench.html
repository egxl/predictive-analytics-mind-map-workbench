Paper Layout Recommendations

1. Landscape Orientation: Turn your paper horizontally so you have plenty of lateral space for algorithms on the left and metrics on the right.
2. Central Box: Write PREDICTION dead center. Leave sufficient padding around it.
3. Colors or Line Patterns: Use a normal pen for solid tree branches and a colored pen (or dashed lines) for relational joins to prevent visual tangling.
