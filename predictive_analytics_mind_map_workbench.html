How To Avoid Overlaps On Paper

1. Keep Left Margin for Highway Lanes: Leave a 1.5-inch strip of empty space on the left edge of your paper. All algorithm lines turn into this strip, travel vertically without crossing each other, and enter the questions from the left!
2. Draw Overhead Arcs Above Center: Pull metric dashed lines up and over the top of the paper (above PREDICTION), so they never intersect the center hierarchy lines.
3. Concentric Radii: Draw each overhead arch slightly higher than the last (like concentric rainbow arches). They will remain completely parallel with zero crossings.
