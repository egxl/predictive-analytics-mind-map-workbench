How to Avoid Overlapping with a Pen

1. Position Strategy: Place Questions on the top-left, Algorithms down the middle-left, and Technique Families at the bottom-left. That way, all algorithm joins remain on the left margin without crossing the center.
2. Pen Colors: Use a black pen for solid tree boxes/lines, and an orange/blue colored pencil or dashed pen for the relationship joins.
3. Overhead Arc for Metrics: Instead of drawing lines straight through the central word PREDICTION, draw curved arches over the top of the paper to reach Classification and Regression.

Metrics Connections

To Classification (Discrete Yes/No):
  • Accuracy: (TP + TN) / All
  • Precision: TP / (TP + FP)
  • Recall: TP / (TP + FN)
  • Specificity: TN / (TN + FP)
  • NPV: TN / (TN + FN)
  • F1-Score: 2*P*R / (P + R)
To Regression & Time Series (Continuous / Gap):
  • MAE: avg(|gap|)
  • MSE: avg(gap^2)
  • RMSE: sqrt(MSE)
  • MAPE: avg(|gap| / actual)
  • R²: 1 - (SS_res / SS_tot) (Regression)
