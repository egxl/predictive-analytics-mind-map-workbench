Why The 4-Corner Layout is Best for Hand Drawing

1. Clean Quadrants: Place PREDICTION in the center, and branch out diagonally to the 4 corners: Algorithms (Top-Left), Family (Bottom-Left), Questions (Top-Right), Metrics (Bottom-Right).
2. Three Pure Border Paths:
  • West margin: Draw lines down from Algorithms to Families.
  • North margin: Draw lines right from Algorithms to Questions.
  • East margin: Draw lines up from Metrics to Questions.
3. No Line Crosses The Middle: None of the dashed pencil lines will ever cut through PREDICTION or intersect any diagonal branch lines!
