Metrics Efficiency Consolidation

Classification Suite: 1 line (saves 5)

All 6 metrics evaluate discrete classification:

  • Accuracy (TP+TN)/all
  • Precision TP/(TP+FP)
  • Recall TP/(TP+FN)
  • Specificity TN/(TN+FP)
  • NPV TN/(TN+FN)
  • F1-Score 2PR/(P+R)
Continuous Error Suite: 2 lines (saves 6)

Evaluate continuous deviations (Regression & TS):

  • MAE avg(|gap|) (Reg & TS)
  • MSE avg(gap^2) (Reg & TS)
  • RMSE sqrt(MSE) (Reg & TS)
  • MAPE avg(|gap|/y) (Reg & TS)
  • R² (1 line to Regression only)
